Character note (fictional):

The player is a white geometric mascot who interprets the arena through rigid categories. He believes his own color represents order and treats every unfamiliar shape as evidence of a conspiracy. The game should present these claims in first person, as thoughts he sincerely believes while under pressure.

Important framing:

- His worldview is biased, paranoid, and unreliable.
- The story may name his worldview as racism and criticize it directly.
- His claims are his own ideas, not facts established by the game.
- Anti-Black racism is a real-world harm; the game must not present it as justified.
- The other shapes are fictional geometric opponents, not representations of real-world racial groups.
- The end-of-run reflection should let his own realization question his conclusions; no separate narrator warning is needed there.
